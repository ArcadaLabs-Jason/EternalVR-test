# EternalVR test builds

Builds of EternalVR that haven't been released yet, for testers. The real releases live at
[ArcadaLabs-Jason/EternalVR](https://github.com/ArcadaLabs-Jason/EternalVR/releases).

**These can break.** They passed my automated checks but nobody has played them in a headset yet, which
is what you're helping with. Keep a normal release around if you want a known-good build.

## How to test one

1. Download the zip from the newest release here and unzip it to its own folder (not over your normal
   install).
2. Run `EternalVR.Launcher.exe` from that folder. Your settings and saves are shared with your normal
   install.
3. The release text is a short list of things to check. Do whichever ones you can; skipping is fine.
4. When you're done, click **Export report** in the launcher and post the zip in the EternalVR thread on
   the [Flat2VR Discord](https://discord.gg/ZFSCSDe) with a line per item: OK, not OK, or didn't try, and
   anything you noticed.

Captures help a lot: in the headset, left Menu + trigger saves what you see, and the export includes it.

Thanks for testing!
